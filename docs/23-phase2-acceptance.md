# 23 — Phase 2 acceptance (P2-21)

**Measured 2026-09-27 on `feature/observer-ux-overhaul-phase2`. Phase 2 is not accepted.** Every one
of the thirteen screens the plan was drawn from has a real route, a real after screenshot and
end-to-end tests that ran green today. But one clause of the gate fails outright — the sales
formulas are not shared: "attention for its share of stock" is computed three ways, and one route
prints a hard-coded 2.1 — and of the 88 Phase 1 and Phase 2 items in the plan's route checklists, 33 are verified, 32 hold in part, 12 are not done and 11 are blocked on a named missing input. UI_READY
is not DONE, and a partial handover is not a claim of product readiness.

Three defects the acceptance found were corrected on the way, each as its own commit with a test:
another developer's agents named on Kingsford Yard's Presentation DNA (`1b5f0e4`); a feature pairing
stated below the floor its own table holds to (`7ffbfb2`); and an attention card that opened the
whole meetings register instead of the meetings it counted (`24e573e`). A fourth, Kingsford's price filters written in euros, was found while checking a screen (`c9d89e5`). After the first measurement three more items were closed: the DNA's chosen pair survives a mode switch and its lede is true (`b226f15`), the comparison's evidence opens the comparison it rests on (`c10616f`), and the agents roster no longer credits a CRM it did not read (`aeb1946`); and the compact lanes' codes got the key their comment promised (`40957fa`). Sales Flow's windows stopped printing 0 for the part of the calendar a period does not hold (`633bf12`).
